The Elder's Basement — When the Chains Break

Chapter One, §2.4 location 5. Balanced for six neonates.

4 combatants + all saved players Cornelius Vandermeer · Mrs. Agnes Holloway · Brutus · Caesar

▶ Start in imagineVTT Tracker only scene: The Elder's Basement - When the Chains Break encounters/set-01-elders-basement.json
Combat notes

Chapter One, §2.4 location 5. Balanced for six neonates.

The place

The cellar of Cornelius Vandermeer's brownstone in Brooklyn Heights: whitewashed brick, a coal boiler, wine racks, and four iron rings bolted into the north wall with chains run through them. One narrow stair up to the kitchen; a coal chute to the garden. A single bulb on a cord.

  • The stair is one person wide. Whoever holds it holds the cellar.
  • The bulb goes out the first time anything heavy hits the ceiling (Storyteller's call): blind fighting, +2 difficulty, until someone finds the kitchen switch.
  • The party wall. Next door, Mr. Feld — a retired schoolteacher with insomnia — has the Night Owl's show on. He only listens on his sleepless nights, which is why Vandermeer's bad nights come only sometimes, and always "around three". At three in the morning the Chant comes through the brick.

The situation

The coterie agreed — through Preston — to watch Vandermeer through a bad night. At 3 a.m. the Chant leaks through the party wall, the Dread takes him, and he tears one chain out of the brick. He is not trying to kill anyone. He is trying to get away from a sound that is inside the walls, and the coterie is between him and the stair.

  • Do not destroy him. He is the only one in the city who can name the voice on the tape. If he meets Final Death, so does that lead (the Prophet's papers at City Hall still give the town).
  • The radio. While it plays, every Kindred in the cellar is at +1 difficulty to everything. Someone can run next door (one turn out, one turn to deal with the sleepy neighbour, one turn back) and shut it off. That is a Masquerade problem of its own.

Who's who

  • Cornelius Vandermeer — Ventrue elder, in frenzy (he starts with the frenzy status set: he ignores wound penalties). Cannot use Dominate or Presence while frenzied. Fortitude 4 and Stamina 5: beating him down with fists will take all night.
  • Mrs. Agnes Holloway — his housekeeper of sixty years, a ghoul, with his shotgun. Starts inactive upstairs. Bring her in at the start of turn 2.
  • Brutus and Caesar — his ghouled mastiffs, kennelled in the garden. Start inactive. Bring them in through the coal chute at the start of turn 3 if the fight is still going.

Special rules

  • Winning without killing. Any of these ends the fight: re-chain him (two characters hold him with a Hold maneuver for a full turn while a third locks a shackle: Dexterity + Security or Crafts, difficulty 7); stake him (heart, difficulty 9, three damage successes); or outlast the Dread — after the radio is off, he rolls Self-Control (difficulty 7) at the end of each turn and comes back to himself on any success.
  • Mrs. Holloway chooses a side. She arrives aiming at whoever is hurting him. If the coterie is plainly trying to restrain him, she lowers the gun and throws them the shackle keys instead (use ✎ to set her side to pc, or simply have her stop shooting). If anyone draws a blade or a gun on him, she fires.
  • The dogs attack whoever is closest to Vandermeer. Mrs. Holloway can call them off (one action).

How it ends

  • When he comes back to himself he is grey, shaking and grateful, and asks what they have learned. Play him side B of the tape and he names the voice — Lysander Ashcroft — and brings out a cabinet photograph of the Court in 1891 with Ashcroft in the second row, and the old ledger: a domain grant in Greene County, the town's name water-damaged. If they don't think to play him the tape, he asks for it: "I have heard every voice at this Court for two hundred years." If they hurt him badly, he still talks, but he remembers who did it.

Fewer players

Delete from the top of this list first:

  • 5 players: delete Caesar.
  • 4 players: also delete Brutus.
  • 3 players: Mrs. Holloway arrives already on the coterie's side (side pc), and Vandermeer starts with 2 bashing — he has been fighting the chains for an hour.

The Hudson Piers — Marisol's Cut

Chapter One, §2.4 location 9. Balanced for six neonates.

7 combatants + all saved players Marisol · Teo · Big Nunzio · Rafferty · Harbour Dog Jib · Harbour Dog Salty · Detective Frank Russo

▶ Start in imagineVTT Tracker only scene: The Hudson Piers - Marisol's Cut encounters/set-02-hudson-piers.json
Combat notes

Chapter One, §2.4 location 9. Balanced for six neonates.

The place

A rotting pier off the Meatpacking District at four in the morning. Gaps in the planking over black water, a collapsed shed, stacked pallets, the West Side Highway roaring behind a chain-link fence. Marisol's people rent boats from the end of it, cash, no questions.

  • The planking. Anyone knocked down near a gap rolls Dexterity + Athletics (difficulty 6) or goes into the Hudson: two turns to climb out, and a strong current.
  • Pallets and the shed give cover (+1 difficulty to ranged attacks).
  • Dawn is coming. The meat trucks start arriving at turn 6: twenty mortal workers and their headlights. After that, everything anyone does is seen.

The situation

The coterie comes to the piers asking about a boat — following Russo's notebook from Bellevue (a boat leaves Marisol's pier every night with people tied in the bottom) or the boat-hire receipt Jinx dropped at the record shop. And once they know about North Brother Island, they need one themselves: it can only be reached by water, and Marisol's people rent boats at night, cash, no questions.

What Marisol is hiding: she has been ferrying the Gravediggers. Hollis paid cash for her boats, then offered more — a place in Sabbat New York when the Court falls. She has taken the pack across to North Brother every night this week. Neonates walking onto her pier with the Sheriff's name, asking who rented her boats, look like the Court finally catching on. For a Camarilla Gangrel helping the Sabbat, that is Final Death. She has to decide whether they leave.

  • It need not be a fight. If the coterie only wants a boat, she rents them one at a steep price and lies about the other customers (Manipulation + Subterfuge 7 dice; a coterie that catches the lie with Perception + Empathy has her cornered).
  • It becomes a fight when they press about the Sabbat, mention reporting to the Sheriff, or catch her lying. Her crew has already drifted round behind them.
  • The duel. Before blood is spilled, Marisol offers Gangrel terms: one of the coterie against her, bare-handed or claws, first to take three health levels loses. If she wins, they forget the questions and take the boat home. If they win, they get the boat and the truth. Her crew honours the result. Refusing the duel starts the fight.

Who's who

  • Marisol — Gangrel, pier boss. Grows Feral Claws (1 blood, one turn) and fights like a dockside animal. Calls her dogs with Animalism on turn 2.
  • Teo, Big Nunzio and Rafferty — her ghoul dockhands, with cargo hooks. Extras: they go down fast, and they try to hook people off the pier.
  • Two harbour dogs — strays she feeds. Start inactive; bring them in at the start of turn 2 (her Animalism call).
  • Detective Frank Russo — NYPD Homicide, working the Cold Storage case and staking out this pier. Starts inactive. See below.

Special rules

  • Russo joins at the first gunshot, or at the start of turn 4. He has been staking out the pier over the fisherman's statement, from a car by the fence. He is a mortal with a revolver and a radio. He shoots at whoever looks like the attacker; with Marisol's ghouls swinging hooks, that is usually them — but he is now a witness to everything. If he sees claws, feeding, or someone taking a bullet and getting up, it is a Masquerade breach the coterie inherits. He calls for backup on the turn he arrives; two patrol cars come at turn 8.
  • Marisol's pride. She never fights to the death: at half health she backs off and talks — see How it ends.

How it ends

  • Duel lost, or Marisol at half health: she gives it all up — the pack's crossings to North Brother Island, the old Tuberculosis Pavilion they haunt, the hours they come and go — and begs the coterie to keep it from the Sheriff. That secret is leverage the coterie keeps for the rest of the chapter.
  • Duel won by Marisol: she rents them a boat home and nothing else. The island will have to be found another way (Benedetta's invitation, or following the pack's boat from the record shop).
  • Either way, the boat that takes the coterie to North Brother leaves from this pier.

Fewer players

Delete from the top of this list first:

  • 5 players: delete Rafferty.
  • 4 players: also delete both harbour dogs, Jib and Salty.
  • 3 players: also delete Big Nunzio. Marisol accepts a single payment instead of a weekly cut.

The SRO — Third Floor

Chapter One, §2.4 location 10. Balanced for six neonates.

8 combatants + all saved players Sister Joy · Tito Ramos · Glenn Hathaway · Benny Okonkwo · Lena Voss · Mr. Abernathy · Kid Dante · Eddie Quinn

▶ Start in imagineVTT Tracker only scene: The SRO - Third Floor encounters/set-03-sro-flophouse.json
Combat notes

Chapter One, §2.4 location 10. Balanced for six neonates.

The place

The third floor of the Hotel Marlowe, a single-room-occupancy hotel on West 47th Street: one long fluorescent hallway, twelve doors, a shared bathroom, the stairwell at one end and a fire-escape window at the other. Mrs. Park watches the lobby from behind bulletproof glass.

  • The hallway is one yard wide. At most two people can fight side by side; ranged attacks past a body in the way are at +1 difficulty.
  • The doors. Every room has someone in it. Most stay shut. Some don't (see the discarded ghouls).
  • Mrs. Park calls 911 at the start of turn 4 if anything is still going on. Patrol cars arrive at turn 8.

The situation

The coterie comes for Eddie Quinn in room 3C. Mrs. Park is paid by the Wakeners to page them when anyone asks for 3C: she takes the coterie's twenty, and as they climb the stairs she picks up the phone (Perception + Alertness, difficulty 6, to notice). About ten minutes later — while the coterie is talking to Eddie through his door — Sister Joy and two of the Faithful come up the stairs to collect him before he says anything more. The fight starts when they reach the third floor.

  • Eddie is the prize. He is the inside man who let the Singers rehearse, and he knows where the church people took him. Whoever leaves with him wins.
  • The smell. The first Kindred to bleed in the hallway wakes the floor: the discarded ghouls in the other rooms have not had vitae in weeks.

Who's who

  • Sister Joy — thin-blood recruiter. Talks first, warmly, and tries to recruit any thin-blood or Caitiff in the coterie on the spot.
  • Tito Ramos and Glenn Hathaway — Caitiff Faithful, there to carry Eddie out.
  • Benny Okonkwo — Wakener driver, waiting in the van downstairs. Starts inactive: comes up the stairs at the start of turn 3.
  • Lena Voss, Mr. Abernathy and Kid Dante — discarded ghouls in withdrawal. Start inactive. See below.
  • Eddie Quinn — starts inactive, behind the door of 3C. See below.

Special rules

  • The hungry floor. Bring in the three discarded ghouls the turn after the first Kindred in the hallway takes lethal or aggravated damage. They are on nobody's side: each turn they go for whichever vampire is bleeding most, on either side, trying to drink rather than kill (a successful bite takes 1 blood from the target instead of damage). Offered vitae, any of them stops fighting.
  • Eddie chooses. He opens the door when someone outside offers him vitae, safety or both (Charisma + Empathy through the door, difficulty 6; Sister Joy rolls for the Wakeners every turn). He joins whoever wins him over (use ✎ to set his side), and fights badly for them with a kitchen knife.
  • Two at a time. Enforce the hallway: anyone not in the front two ranks can only shoot past, use Disciplines, or wait.

How it ends

  • The Wakeners leave when Eddie is lost or Sister Joy goes down; Benny drives off without anyone.
  • Eddie, safe and fed, confesses: the rehearsals, the church people (the soup kitchen), and the old man under the bridge.

Fewer players

Delete from the top of this list first:

  • 5 players: delete Glenn Hathaway.
  • 4 players: also delete Benny Okonkwo and Kid Dante.
  • 3 players: also delete Tito Ramos. Sister Joy came with only the driver.

Cherry Bomb Records — Fire in the Dubbing Room

Chapter One, §2.4 location 12. Balanced for six neonates.

7 combatants + all saved players Hollis · Mister Bone · Jinx · Dot Malloy · Lou Fiorella · Nadia Brandt · The Fire

▶ Start in imagineVTT Tracker only scene: The Record Shop - Fire in the Dubbing Room encounters/set-04-record-shop.json
Combat notes

Chapter One, §2.4 location 12. Balanced for six neonates.

The place

Cherry Bomb Records, Dot Malloy's shop on the ground floor of an old brick warehouse at 81 Cherry Street, in the shadow of the Manhattan Bridge — the industrial edge of the Lower East Side, where the bridge traffic drums overhead all night. About 43 × 24 yards, all on one floor.

  • The shop. A narrow storefront at street level: crates of vinyl, punk flyers, the counter. The breaker and the fire extinguisher are behind the counter.
  • The door. One door from the shop into the warehouse behind it — the choke point. Only two people get through it in a turn.
  • The warehouse. Far more space than Dot's operation needs. The dubbing corner — a bench of high-speed cassette decks playing the Chant, towers of blank-tape boxes, master tapes labelled by date, and under the bench the shoebox and the old man's box — is one corner of a huge, half-empty floor: pallets of unsold records and dead stock, an old printing press, a forklift, and a mezzanine office up a steel stair.
  • The loading dock. A roll-up door to the alley, chained shut from inside. Dot has the key.
  • The Chant. The decks play it at low volume the whole time: every Kindred within earshot of the dubbing corner is at +1 difficulty to everything until someone kills the power (the breaker behind the counter; one action).
  • What burns. The dubbing corner and the stock — paper, vinyl, cardboard and thousands of cassettes. Open concrete floor away from the stock burns slowly or not at all.

The situation

The coterie comes in through the shop after the cult's distribution network and walks in on Dot Malloy and two of the Faithful in the dubbing corner. The Gravediggers have been watching the shop for two nights from a doorway across the street (a coterie that saw Jinx at the Bowery dive may spot him: Perception + Alertness, difficulty 7). When Jinx sees the coterie go in, Hollis decides the Camarilla is not having it, and the pack comes in behind them on turn 2. It becomes a three-way fight, and then the warehouse catches fire.

  • The shoebox. Dot's distribution list — every drop point in the city, the biggest under the Manhattan Bridge, a few minutes' walk away — is in a shoebox under the dubbing bench. Grabbing it is one action at the bench.
  • The Gravediggers' goal is a live Wakener to take back to North Brother Island. Once they have one, they leave the way they came, or by the loading dock if it is open.
  • The old man's box. Under the dubbing bench, beside the shoebox: a carton of cassettes labelled in a copperplate hand (Survey, 1898; Chambers, 1899), all taped over for the cells but one, which plays the old voice from side B of the Cold Storage tape, reading measurements. Taking it is one action. Dot, asked: "The old man brought his recordings in years ago, to be copied off old cylinders. Now he says tape over them." It burns with the shoebox at Spread 4.

Who's who

  • Hollis — Brujah antitribu, the ductus. Starts inactive: in through the shop door at the start of turn 2 — or through the loading dock if it is open, cutting off that escape. Blocks the door while the others work.
  • Mister Bone — Tzimisce. Starts inactive, with Hollis. Wants a specimen, and the coterie is more interesting than the Wakeners.
  • Jinx — Pander. Starts inactive, with Hollis, and hidden (Obfuscate). Sets the fire.
  • Dot Malloy — the shop's owner, a Believer ghoul. Thinks she is helping. Defends the masters with a baseball bat.
  • Lou Fiorella and Nadia Brandt — Caitiff Faithful guarding the dubbing room. They attack whoever is closest to Dot.
  • The Fire — a hazard, not a person. Starts inactive.

Special rules

  • The pack comes in on turn 2, through the shop door, where Hollis plants himself in the doorway. If someone has opened the loading dock, they come in that way instead and cut off the escape.
  • The Fire starts at the end of turn 2 (Jinx's aerosol torch into a tower of blank tapes, or a knocked-over lamp). Bring it in with ▶; it acts last every turn whatever its initiative says.
  • Spread. The Fire's extra pool is Spread. At its turn, add 1. 1–2: the dubbing corner burns. 3: the fire reaches the stock, and smoke fills the roof space: +1 difficulty to everything, everywhere — +2 for anyone on the mezzanine. 4: the shoebox and the old man's box are gone. 5: the stock and the shop door burn (two levels a turn in burning areas). 6: the roof comes down over the dubbing corner and the stock — 4 dice bashing to anyone there, and they must get out next turn.
  • Safe lanes. Open floor away from the stock burns slowly or not at all. The fight can go on there while the corner burns.
  • Burning. At the Fire's turn, use its Burning Room weapon on everyone standing in a burning area (1 aggravated, Fortitude soak at difficulty 5).
  • Putting it out. The extinguisher is behind the counter: one action, Dexterity + Athletics (difficulty 6), each success removes 1 Spread. It only buys time.
  • Rötschreck. The first time a character is in a burning area, roll Courage (difficulty 6): failure means they bolt for the nearest exit — the shop door or the loading dock — through anyone in the way.

How it ends

  • The Gravediggers leave with a wounded Wakener, by car across town to the West Side — to Marisol's pier, which a coterie that follows them will see. In the struggle, Jinx drops a boat-hire receipt from the same piers. Either way, the trail leads to the Hudson piers, and from there to North Brother Island.
  • The shop and the warehouse are lost either way. What the coterie saved — the shoebox, a master tape, the old man's box — is what they have.

Fewer players

Delete from the top of this list first:

  • 5 players: delete Nadia Brandt.
  • 4 players: also delete Lou Fiorella.
  • 3 players: also delete Mister Bone (he is waiting with the car), and the Fire starts at the end of turn 3 instead.

Beneath Canal Street — Hob's Water

Chapter One, §2.4 location 14. Balanced for six neonates.

4 combatants + all saved players Hob · Rat Swarm 1 · Rat Swarm 2 · Big Al

▶ Start in imagineVTT Tracker only scene: Beneath Canal Street - Hob's Water encounters/set-05-canal-street-sewers.json
Combat notes

Chapter One, §2.4 location 14. Balanced for six neonates.

The place

The brick drainage tunnels under Canal Street, built over the old Collect Pond. Knee-deep black water, arched ceilings low enough to touch, side passages every few yards, and the walls humming exactly as Mister Tuesday said. No light except what the coterie brings.

  • The water costs a die from every Dexterity pool (Dodge, Brawl, Melee, Firearms) and halves movement. Nobody can light anything that isn't held above it.
  • Darkness. Without a light source: blind fighting, +2 difficulty. Hob and the rats don't need light.
  • The storm. A summer thunderstorm is breaking over Manhattan. From turn 4 the water rises: another −1 die each turn (to −3). At turn 7 the tunnel floods to the ceiling — swim out or be swept toward the river.

The situation

The coterie goes looking for Sexton, Pretty Nicky's broodmate, who has gone to ground down here. Hob, his enormous mute ghoul, has decided that anyone coming down the Canal Street hatch is a Wakener or a Gravedigger. He does not ask.

  • Sexton is listening from a side passage. He is terrified, not hostile. He can call Hob off.

Who's who

  • Hob — Sexton's ghoul guard, an ex-sandhog. Mute, huge, patient. Knows every trap because he set them.
  • Two rat swarms — Sexton's warren-rats, Nosferatu-fed. Hob's tripwire: they come first.
  • Big Al — the sewer alligator. Every New Yorker has heard the story; Sexton has been feeding this one vitae for eleven years. Starts inactive.

Special rules

  • Calling off Hob. Anyone who shouts into the dark that they come from Pretty Nicky, or offers a copy of the tape or news from above, rolls Charisma + Empathy (difficulty 6; 5 if Nicky is with them). On success Sexton whistles from the side passage, and Hob and the rats stop. Big Al does not listen to anyone.
  • Traps. Each turn someone moves more than a few yards, they roll Perception + Alertness (difficulty 7) or hit one: a deadfall of bricks (4 dice bashing), a tripwire bell (Hob knows exactly where they are: +1 die to his next attack), or a hidden drop into the old pond (knocked down, under water for a turn).
  • Swarms follow the Revised swarm rules (on their weapon): pistols, knives and fists remove only one health level per hit; shotguns, large weapons and fire do full damage. A swarm is dispersed when its boxes are full.
  • Big Al wakes at the start of the turn after anyone bleeds into the water (any lethal or aggravated damage). He goes for whoever is lowest in the water — knocked down or overrun first.

How it ends

  • Called off, Hob leads them to Sexton, who trades where (the stretch of line near City Hall) for the tape or news from above.
  • If Hob is destroyed, Sexton flees deeper and the coterie has to find his hand-drawn map in his empty nest — after the water rises.

Fewer players

Delete from the top of this list first:

  • 5 players: delete Rat Swarm 2.
  • 4 players: also delete Big Al.
  • 3 players: also delete Rat Swarm 1, and the storm starts at turn 6 instead of turn 4.

North Brother Island — Mister Bone's Ward

Chapter One, §2.4 location 18. Balanced for six neonates.

9 combatants + all saved players Mister Bone · Hollis · Jinx · Gil, Remade · Hannah, Remade · Dory, Remade · The Ward Thing · The Quarantine Dead · Tobias

▶ Start in imagineVTT Tracker only scene: North Brother Island - Mister Bone's Ward encounters/set-06-north-brother-island.json
Combat notes

Chapter One, §2.4 location 18. Balanced for six neonates.

The place

An abandoned hospital island in the East River, closed since 1963 and swallowed by ailanthus and vines. Typhoid Mary died in quarantine here, and in 1904 the excursion steamer General Slocum burned in Hell Gate and was run aground on its shore — over a thousand dead in sight of the island, most of them women and children. The fight happens in the old Tuberculosis Pavilion: a long ward on the ground floor, rusted iron bedframes in rows, a collapsed skylight, ankle-deep leaf rot.

  • Light: none, except the Gravediggers' kerosene lamps on a surgical trolley at the far end. Knock the trolley over and the ward is dark (blind fighting: +2 difficulty) and a lamp fire starts — torch-sized, heat 5.
  • The stair: a concrete stair at the near end leads to the second-floor nurses' station. The Ward Thing cannot climb it.
  • Bedframes: cover (+1 difficulty to ranged attacks through the rows) and difficult ground for anyone charging (half movement down the aisles).
  • The river: the pack's boat is tied at the old ferry slip, two minutes' run from the pavilion doors.

The situation

The coterie arrives — by Marisol's boat, or following the pack's — in the middle of something. The Gravediggers have spent the night interrogating Wakeners, and Mister Bone has been making things. Two of his guards are fresh from a fight with a Wakener ghoul who got loose, and the island's dead have started waking in the old wards. Nobody here is at full strength, which is the only reason the coterie can win this.

  • Tobias, the half-reshaped Singer the coterie needs (he knows where the faithful gather), is strapped to a bedframe at the far end, under the lamps. He is on the map as a token (immobilized), but he is not a combatant: he does not act, and nobody should target him. Anyone who reaches him and asks gets his whisper.
  • If the coterie already has a truce with the Gravediggers (Coney Island), this is not a fight at first: Benedetta's invitation stands, and Mister Bone shows them his work. It turns into one the moment someone objects to what is on the tables — or when the Quarantine Dead arrive.

Who's who

  • Mister Bone (Tzimisce) — the host. Wants to keep his guests, not kill them. Opens with Bonecraft on the nearest neonate and goes into Horrid Form once he is hurt (2 blood, one turn: +3 to all Physical; use the card's blood boosts).
  • Hollis (Brujah antitribu) — the ductus. One shell left. Wants New York, not this fight: once two of the pack's side are down, he leaves for the boat and takes Jinx.
  • Jinx (Pander) — starts Obfuscated at the trolley. The one who could be turned: anyone who saw him at the Bowery dive and offers him a way out gets a chance (Manipulation + Empathy, difficulty 7). If it works, he lowers the gun and kicks over the lamps on his way out.
  • Gil, Hannah and Dory, Remade — captured Bound Wakeners reshaped into guards, blood-bound to Mister Bone with the Binding still underneath. They weep while they fight. Anyone humming the Chant makes every Remade within earshot stop for a turn and sing along — and gives every Kindred in the room a Dread roll (Courage, difficulty 6).
  • The Ward Thing — two orderlies fused into one body. Nine health levels, armor 2, three bile sacs. Ignores its first wound penalty. Cannot climb stairs.
  • The Quarantine Dead — not on anyone's side, and not there yet: it starts inactive. See below.

Special rules

  • The Quarantine Dead starts inactive. Bring it in with ▶ at the start of the turn after the first frenzy or first death in the ward, or at the start of turn 3 at the latest.
  • Dead — target: each turn roll a die — 1–5 the most wounded combatant on the coterie's side, 6–10 the most wounded on the Gravediggers' side. Vampires need Auspex to see it; without it, attacks against it are at +2 difficulty.
  • Dead — spending Passion (click dots off its card): manifest costs 1 per turn and is needed to do anything at all; unmanifested it is an unseen chill that cannot act or be hit. Grave-cold touch costs nothing extra. Fling the ward costs +1. The fever dream costs +2: instead of attacking, one target rolls Courage (difficulty 7) — a vampire who fails is in Rötschreck and flees the ward for the turn; a ghoul or mortal freezes and loses the turn.
  • Dead — gaining Passion: +1 whenever anyone frenzies, +1 whenever anyone dies in the ward. It is a clock that runs slower the uglier the fight gets.
  • Dead — fading: it fades if it starts a turn on 0 Passion, or when its health runs out. It starts on 4 — about three or four turns, unless the fight feeds it.
  • Fleshcraft. If Mister Bone wins a grapple, he can spend his action on Dexterity + Body Crafts (difficulty 5) instead of damage: each success lowers the victim's Appearance by one. It is not lethal. It is worse.
  • The bone saw. At the Storyteller's call, one level from it heals only with blood, never by rest.
  • Fire. The lamps, Jinx's aerosol torch and any flare are the most dangerous things in the room for everyone. Call Rötschreck rolls when fire comes out (Courage; difficulty 6 for a torch nearby, higher if it is pointed at someone).

How it ends

  • Mister Bone at half health offers a trade: Tobias, and the coterie's lives, for being left alone. He means it.
  • Hollis leaves once two of the pack's side are down (the Remade and the Ward Thing count). The coterie may let the boat go — or lose it.
  • Win condition is not killing everyone: it is reaching Tobias and hearing where. Everything after that is a choice.

Fewer players

Delete from the top of this list first:

  • 5 players: delete Dory, Remade.
  • 4 players: also delete Hannah, Remade, and drop the Bile Gland from The Ward Thing (✕ Drop this weapon).
  • 3 players: also delete Gil, Remade. Hollis leaves at the end of turn 1 whatever happens, and set the Quarantine Dead's Passion to 2.
  • 2 players or fewer: don't run it as a fight. Run the truce version, and let the Quarantine Dead be the danger.

City Hall Station — The Sermon

Chapter One, §2.4 location 19. Balanced for six neonates.

17 combatants + all saved players Brother Calloway · Singer Hope Delaney · Singer Abe Kessler · Singer Lucía Paz · Singer Ruth Abernathy · Theo Castellano · Rafe Delacroix · Ines Varga · Big Sol Mercer · Delphine Okoro · Hollis · Sister Benedetta · Mister Bone · Jinx · Abyssal Tentacle 1 · Abyssal Tentacle 2 · Abyssal Tentacle 3

▶ Start in imagineVTT Tracker only scene: City Hall Station - The Sermon encounters/set-07-city-hall-station.json
Combat notes

Chapter One, §2.4 location 19. Balanced for six neonates.

The place

City Hall station, closed since 1945: Guastavino tile vaults, brass chandeliers, leaded skylights painted black, and hundreds of candles on every ledge. A choir of the discarded on the curved platform. The Prophet in an alcove at the far end, behind a lectern made from a ticket booth.

  • The 6 train loops through to turn around. At the start of every third turn it passes: the station shakes, floods with light, and for that turn nobody can hear the Chant and ranged attacks are at +2 difficulty from the glare.
  • The third rail runs along the curve. Anyone knocked or thrown off the platform rolls Dexterity + Athletics (difficulty 6) to avoid it: 8 dice of bashing, soaked normally (the Storyteller's number).
  • Candles. Every knockdown near a ledge sets something alight (Storyteller's call). Torch-sized fire.
